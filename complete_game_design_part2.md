- **Gender Balance**: Approximately 65% male, 35% female
- **Geographic Priority**: North America, Europe, Southeast Asia, Japan, Korea
- **Platform Users**: Smartphone and tablet owners
- **Spending Capacity**: Mix of F2P, low spenders, and whales

#### Psychographic Profiles
- **The Collector**: Motivated by completing collections
- **The Competitor**: Driven by ranking and achievement
- **The Socializer**: Values community and cooperation
- **The Explorer**: Enjoys discovering game world and lore
- **The Achiever**: Focused on progression and completion

#### Player Journey Mapping
- **Discovery Phase**: How potential players first learn about game
- **Onboarding Experience**: First impression and tutorial
- **Engagement Deepening**: Transition to regular play
- **Monetization Introduction**: First spending opportunity
- **Long-term Retention**: Keeping players for months/years
